# Outreach Messages — copy, personalise, send

Three versions: email, LinkedIn/Facebook message, phone opener. Personalise only the parts in `[square brackets]`. Everything else is deliberately plain — polished marketing copy gets deleted; a short, honest ask from a real person gets answered.

Notes before sending:

- **No product name is used anywhere.** We have a working name but it's internal-only, and naming a product makes this sound like a sales call, which kills honesty.
- **The ask is 20 minutes.** Small enough to say yes to, long enough to cover the essential questions.
- **Expect silence.** Sending 40 and hearing back from 12 is a *good* result. Silence is not rejection; it's a busy person. One follow-up per person, then stop.

---

## 1. Email

**Subject:** `Quick question about NDIS claiming — not selling anything`

> Hi [first name],
>
> I'm [your name], based in [city]. I'm researching a problem I keep hearing about from NDIS providers: claims getting rejected or cancelled after the work's already been done, and the scramble to get evidence together when an audit comes around.
>
> Before building anything, I'm trying to find out whether that matches reality for businesses like [organisation name] — or whether I've got it wrong.
>
> Could I get 20 minutes on the phone in the next week or two? I'm not selling anything — there's nothing to sell — I just want to hear how claiming and audit prep actually work for you, and what's annoying about it.
>
> Happy to work around your schedule. And if this lands with the wrong person, I'd be grateful for a point in the right direction.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]
> [phone number]

**Follow-up (send once, 4–5 business days later, same thread):**

> Hi [first name] — just floating this back up. 20 minutes, nothing being sold, genuinely just research. If it's a no, that's completely fine, and a one-word "no" reply saves us both the guessing.
>
> [your name]

## 2. LinkedIn connection request / Facebook DM

(LinkedIn caps connection notes at 300 characters — this fits.)

> Hi [first name] — I'm researching how NDIS providers handle claim rejections and audit prep, before deciding whether to build something for it. Not selling anything. Would you have 20 min for a call? Happy to fit your schedule.

**If they accept but don't reply, one nudge:**

> Thanks for connecting! No pressure at all — if a 20-minute chat about how claiming/audits work at [organisation] is ever doable, I'd really value it. Your experience would directly shape whether this thing should exist.

**Facebook variant when messaging someone who posted a complaint** (reference their post, never quote it back at them publicly):

> Hi [first name] — I saw your post in [group name] about [claims being rejected / audit prep]. I'm researching exactly that problem before deciding whether to build a tool for it — not selling anything. Would you have 20 minutes for a call? What you've been through is precisely what I need to understand.

## 3. Phone opener (for numbers from the Provider Finder)

Ring mid-morning or mid-afternoon; avoid 9am and end-of-day. Have the interview script open in case they say "now is fine".

> "Hi, my name's [your name] — I'm doing research into how NDIS providers deal with claims and audits. I'm not selling anything, I promise — I'm trying to work out whether a problem I keep hearing about is real before anyone builds anything. Could I ask who handles your NDIS claiming — and whether they'd be open to a 20-minute chat sometime this week or next?"

If the person answering *is* the right person and offers to talk now: "That would be brilliant — I'll keep it to 20 minutes," then go straight to the script.

If gatekeeper takes a message: get the right person's name and email, and send the email version referencing the call: "I rang earlier today and [gatekeeper name] kindly suggested I email you…"

## 4. Consultant variant (email)

> Hi [first name],
>
> I'm [your name] — I'm researching the claims-rejection and audit-preparation problem for small and mid-sized NDIS providers, before deciding whether to build software for it.
>
> You see across dozens of providers, which is a view no single provider can give me. Could I get 30 minutes to hear what you see go wrong most often with claiming and audit evidence? Not selling anything, and happy to share back what I learn from the research — that's the only thing I have to offer right now.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]

## What to do with every response

Log it in the CSV (`response` column: `yes` / `no` / `silence` / `referred`). Book calls directly into your calendar and put the date in `interview_date`. Then open `interview-script.md` and, before the first real call, do the rehearsal (see `README.md`).
