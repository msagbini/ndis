# Scoring Sheet — filled in by the agent, not the founder

The founder hands over raw notes (see `note-taking.md`); the agent scores each interview here and computes the verdict against `docs/go-no-go.md`. The founder's role is to sanity-check the verdict, not to do arithmetic.

## Scoring definitions (fixed before any interview is scored)

**PAIN = yes** when the notes show, in the last 12 months, at least one of:
- a claim rejected, cancelled, or clawed back (Q5 = yes with any concrete detail), **or**
- audit preparation described with words like painful/stressful/weeks of work/paid a consultant/nights and weekends (Q8) — the interviewee's own characterisation, not our interpretation. A merely-neutral "we did an audit, it was fine" is **no**.

**SPEND = yes** when Q10's monthly total is A$150 or more. "Rough" estimates count at face value; "guess" counts only if ≥ A$300 (margin for error). Missing number = **no** (and a follow-up email is worth sending).

**COMPLETED = yes** when all ★ questions (Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5, Q6, Q8, Q10) have answers.

Edge rules: one organisation = one row even if two people were on the call. Consultant interviews are recorded below for insight but **excluded** from the percentages — the threshold is about buyers.
