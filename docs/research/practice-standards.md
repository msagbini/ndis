# NDIS Practice Standards, Quality Indicators, and Audits

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-27. Same method and labels as the rest of the dossier (**[OFFICIAL]** = Commission/legislation/government page, **[WEAK]** = auditor/consultant/vendor, **[UNVERIFIED]**): direct fetches were proxy-blocked; facts come from search retrieval of the cited pages.

## Plain-English summary

To be a registered NDIS provider you must meet the **NDIS Practice Standards** — quality rules set under legislation — and prove it in recurring third-party **audits**. Auditors check documented evidence: policies, worker screening records, incident registers, participant records. Our Stage 3 product ("evidence gaps + audit pack") maps a provider's records to the specific indicators an auditor samples. Important caveat for product planning: **the standards themselves are mid-reform** — a KPMG-led review is replacing the structure, and new registration rules started taking effect from 1 July 2026 — so our evidence-mapping must be built against versioned standards, not hard-coded ones.

## The legal instruments [OFFICIAL]

- Standards are made under the **NDIS (Provider Registration and Practice Standards) Rules 2018** — Federal Register of Legislation ID **F2018L00631**.
- The detailed indicators are in the **NDIS (Quality Indicators for NDIS Practice Standards) Guidelines 2018** (F2018N00041; a 2026 compilation exists following a Supported Independent Living amendment — compilation ID reported as F2026C00528 [UNVERIFIED — register page not opened]).
- The Commission's consolidated guidance PDF: **"NDIS Practice Standards and Quality Indicators", November 2021, Version 4** — this is the document our `ndis-evidence-mapper` skill would parse. https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-10/ndis-practice-standards-and-quality-indicators.pdf
- Total count of quality indicators: **[UNVERIFIED]** — no retrieved source stated a number; count them from the PDF itself before Stage 3.

## Structure [OFFICIAL]

- **Core module** (higher-risk supports), four divisions: Rights and Responsibilities; Governance and Operational Management; Provision of Supports; Support Provision Environment.
- **Supplementary modules:** 1 High Intensity Daily Personal Activities; 2 Specialist Behaviour Support; 2A Implementing Behaviour Support Plans; 3 Early Childhood Supports; 4 Specialised Support Coordination; 5 Specialist Disability Accommodation; and from **1 July 2026**, a new **Supported Independent Living (SIL)** module.
- **Verification module** for lower-risk supports.

## Certification vs verification [OFFICIAL unless noted]

- **Verification audit** = low-risk supports (e.g. most sole-trader therapists): single-stage desktop review of documents. The Commission publishes exactly what's required: **"Verification Module – Required Documentation" (May 2025)** — worker qualifications, screening clearances, insurance certificates of currency, and policies for incident management, complaints, risk, HR, WHS.
- **Certification audit** = higher-risk supports (our 5–50-staff target segment): **Stage 1** desktop document review, then **Stage 2 on-site** — service locations, staff interviews, **participant records**, with participant interviews on an opt-out sampling basis [WEAK for the opt-out detail].
- **Cycle:** registration runs ~3 years with a **mid-term audit at 18 months** for certification-pathway providers [OFFICIAL for mid-term; 3-year validity phrasing via auditor sites, WEAK]. This cadence means audit prep is a *recurring* cost, not a one-off — good for a subscription product.
- Audits are performed by **approved quality auditors** under the NDIS (Approved Quality Auditors Scheme) Guidelines 2018 (F2018N00114), which include **"Annex B — Audit methodology and sampling"** — the closest thing to a published sampling spec; obtain it before Stage 3 [OFFICIAL].
- The **scope of audit document** is auto-generated by the Commission from the provider's registration groups — i.e. which standards apply to which provider is determined by their registration groups [OFFICIAL]. Our data model should mirror that: registration groups → applicable modules → indicators → evidence.

## Reform in flight (2025–2026) — build for change [OFFICIAL unless noted]

- **Practice Standards reform:** KPMG-led national review, consultation from March 2025; proposed replacement of the core module with four "core practice domains" (individual rights; provider leadership; safe support practice; effective and impactful support) [WEAK — Team DSC summary]. A Supported Decision Making standard was field-tested Feb–Mar 2026.
- **Mandatory registration expansion:** SIL providers and digital platform providers must register from **1 July 2026** (SIL with new SIL Practice Standards); support-coordination mandatory registration **paused**.
- **Provider definition consultation** ("Getting It Right") closed 28 Feb 2026 — risk-based registration tiers coming.
- **NDIS Amendment (Securing the NDIS for Future Generations) Act 2026**: passed 19 Aug 2026, assent 20 Aug 2026, first changes commencing **27 Aug 2026** (see `record-keeping.md` for the record-keeping impact).
- The Commission publishes a **Regulatory Reform Roadmap (Feb 2026)** — track it each quarter.

**Product implications:** (1) more registered providers = larger addressable market for certification-pathway tooling; (2) standards/indicators must be versioned entities in our schema (same lesson as pricing); (3) SIL providers registering for the first time in 2026-27 are a fresh, underserved audit-prep segment.
